DO NOT BUY SEPARATELY IF BUYING K2402
=====================================

1. K2112 soil moisture sensor
   Original code: 74854
   Reason:
   K2402 already includes 4 capacitive soil moisture sensors, which are better
   for this project than the resistive K2112 probe.

2. Standalone 3-5 V / 200 mA pump
   Original code: 88738
   Reason:
   K2402 already includes 4 submersible water pumps.

3. IRLZ44N
   Original code: 36316
   Reason:
   TU stock was 0. Use IRLB3034 instead.

4. K010 liquid-level controller
   Original code: 41823
   Reason:
   It needs 10-16 V and is not suitable for the 3.7 V / 5 V ESP32 plant node.
   Use XSL-4510-P instead.

5. K2402 relay module
   Reason:
   It is included in the kit, but the final design should use MOSFET low-side
   switching for the pump, not the relay board.

SUMMARY
=======

Best TU-store strategy:
- Buy K2402 for soil sensors, pumps, hose, breadboard, and jumper wires.
- Buy K2156, K548, K585, XSL-4510-P, IRLB3034, 1N5819, 470uF capacitor,
  and connector cables separately.
- Keep the final circuit as ESP32 + LiPo charger + boost converter + MOSFET.
- Do not redesign around the UNO or relay board from K2402.
